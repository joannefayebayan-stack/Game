🔎 Odd One Out

One icon in the grid is different. Find it before the clock runs out! Across 50 levels the grid gets bigger, the timer gets shorter, and the differences get sneakier. You have just 3 lives. A wrong click or a timeout costs a life. Later levels add visual noise (every icon is slightly jittered) and the grid even shuffles while you search.
